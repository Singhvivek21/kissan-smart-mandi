KISSAN: Procure Smart Mandi

Digital Slot Booking, Vector QR Gate Passes, Live Yard Traffic Control & Instant Form 'J' MSP Payouts
Problem in One Sentence: Indian farmers face chaotic 12–24 hour queues outside APMC Mandis with middlemen exploitation and paperwork delays. KISSAN replaces manual congestion with a streamlined, digital end-to-end ecosystem: Slot Booking -> Dynamic QR Pass -> Camera Scan Gate In -> Live Yard Queue -> Weighbridge Scale -> Form 'J' DBT Receipt.

The Traditional Mandi Problem

  • Massive Yard Congestion: 12–24 hour tractor traffic jams during peak harvest days.
  • Middlemen & Underweighing: Lack of digital weighbridge transparency.
  • Paperwork Friction: Manual physical Form 'J' slips get lost or delayed.
  • No Pre-Arrival Scheduling: Thousands of farmers arrive simultaneously.

The KISSAN Smart Solution

  • Pre-Scheduled Arrival Slots: 3 balanced daily windows (Morning, Noon, Afternoon).
  • Zero-Delay Vector QR Passes: Instant, tamper-proof entry passes.
  • Mobile Camera Gate Scanner: High-speed check-in without costly hardware.
  • Automated Form 'J' & MSP: Direct calculations with DBT bank audit trail.

1. Complete Technology Stack & Architecture

Layer Technology Used Core Purpose & Advantage
Frontend UI HTML5 + Modern CSS3 Zero-dependency, ultra-lightweight, 100% responsive, renders smoothly on low-bandwidth rural 2G/3G mobile devices.
Client Logic Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+) No heavy framework overhead (No React/Angular/Vue), instantaneous browser execution, zero bundle size issues.
Backend & Database Supabase (PostgreSQL 15) Scalable relational database with Row Level Security (RLS), real-time subscriptions, and auto-generated REST APIs.
Authentication Supabase Auth (RBAC) Role-Based Access Control cryptographically distinguishing FARMER vs OFFICER/ADMIN workflows.
QR Code Engine Custom SVG Engine (KissanQR) 100% Offline-Ready vector SVG QR generation in 0ms; handles regional UTF-8 crop names without CDN latency.
Computer Vision HTML5-QRCode Camera Scanner Turns any smartphone/tablet camera or gate terminal webcam into an instant high-speed QR pass validator.
State & Cache KissanDB (Hybrid Cache) Dual-layer offline-first architecture; persists bookings locally and synchronizes bidirectionally with Supabase.
Export & Compliance CSS Print Engine (@media print) Generates standard government-compliant A4 and thermal printable Gate Passes and official Form 'J' Sale Slips.

2. How the System Works (End-to-End Workflow)

1
Farmer Profile & Authentication

Farmer logs in via Supabase Auth. Profile details (Name, Mobile, Landholding, District) are safely retrieved via RLS.

2
Smart Slot Booking & Instant QR Pass Generation

Farmer selects Mandi hub, crop (e.g. Wheat @ ₹2,275/Qtl), quantity, and preferred arrival window. The system assigns a unique Token (KMN-046) and instantly renders a tamper-proof SVG QR Code.

3
Mandi Gate 1: Live Mobile Camera Scanning & Admission

The gate security officer points their smartphone/tablet camera at the farmer's pass. The system decodes the payload, verifies booking authenticity, and admits the tractor into the yard with 1 click.

4
Real-Time Digital Yard Queue Board

Vehicles move systematically across 3 stages: Gate Entry -> Yard Transit -> Weighbridge Scale. Farmers can view live "Now Serving Token" numbers from their phones.

5
Digital Weighbridge & Moisture Quality Grading

Mandi operators record Gross Weight, Tare Weight, and Moisture % (FAQ standards). Net Produce Weight and total MSP value are calculated automatically.

6
Digital Form 'J' Mandi Receipt & DBT Settlement

An official Government Form 'J' digital certificate is issued containing the transaction ID, verified QR stamp, and DBT bank reference for immediate payout verification.

3. Key Technical Innovations & USPs

Zero Hardware Barrier

100% Offline QR Engine

Official Form 'J' Slip

Standardized digital procurement receipts with verifiable QR authentication stamps.
